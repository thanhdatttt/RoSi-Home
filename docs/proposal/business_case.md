# Business Case - RosiHome

This business case represents landlords who manage roughly 1-30 rental rooms themselves.

## 1. The Story: Mr. Tuấn's Boarding House

Mr. Tuấn (48, Ho Chi Minh City) owns and manages a 12-room boarding house that he built up over a decade of saving. Every month he walks room to room with a notebook to read the electricity and water meters by hand, then does the rent math on his phone's calculator before texting each tenant on Zalo. His meter readings are in a notebook, rent calculations are in a spreadsheet, lease details are in paper contracts, and maintenance requests are buried in Zalo chats. When a tenant reports a leaking tap or broken light, he has to search old messages to find the room, the photo, and whether the repair was completed. A request can be buried under ordinary messages, leaving him without one reliable list of open issues. Three months later, a tenant's lease quietly expires without Mr. Tuấn noticing - he only finds out when the tenant has already moved out, leaving the room empty for a month with no new tenant lined up.

## 2. How RosiHome Changes the Game

RosiHome replaces the notebook-and-Zalo routine with one simple workflow. Landlords set up bank-transfer information once and enter meter readings for each room during each billing period; RosiHome calculates each invoice, attaches a corresponding QR code for the tenant to scan in their bank app, makes the invoice available to the tenant, and lets the tenant upload payment proof. Lease renewals are tracked and flagged in advance, maintenance requests are logged with photos and status, and a dashboard brings rooms, leases, invoices, maintenance requests, occupancy, and revenue together.

## 3. The Result
 
Mr. Tuấn stops losing time searching notebooks, spreadsheets, and Zalo chats, and reduces the risk of vacancies caused by missed renewal dates. RosiHome handles the arithmetic, reminders, and maintenance record-keeping, reducing repetitive paperwork while Mr. Tuấn still verifies bank transfers himself.
